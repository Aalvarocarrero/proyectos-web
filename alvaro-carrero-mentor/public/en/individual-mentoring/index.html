INDIVIDUAL MENTORING · IN DEVELOPMENT

Your own direction, with support.

A one-to-one space to explore a priority, choose realistic actions and reflect on what you learn by putting them into practice.

For adults aged 18 and over. Spanish will be the primary language.

Notify me about individual mentoring

You can request email updates and confirm your signup with Kit. Expressing interest does not mean making a purchase or reserving a place.

How it may help

This may interest you if you would like a private space to think through a decision or adapt a habit to your responsibilities.

The starting point is to clarify what matters and try an action that fits your everyday life.

Proposed format, pending validation

Personalized support. The first pilot is planned for a maximum of three participants, so each person’s process can receive dedicated attention.

This is the planned capacity for a pilot in preparation, not an announcement of available places.

A six-week process, with one 50-minute individual session each week: six sessions in total.

This is a proposal in development, subject to validation. Pricing, terms and dates will be defined before enrollment opens. Places and reservations are not yet available.

A proposed six-step journey

This process is being prepared and will be refined as the proposal is validated. The practices do not guarantee results, and you can adapt them to your circumstances.

  1. Agree on a priority and how to notice what you are learning.
  2. Explore your values and the criteria that guide your decisions.
  3. Design a realistic action that fits your circumstances.
  4. Review obstacles and adjust the action when needed.
  5. Practice following through and recognize when to seek appropriate help.
  6. Reflect on what you have learned and define your next steps.

Proposed materials and support

The proposal includes a priority map, a weekly plan, a practice log and a closing review to help you plan how to continue.

A weekly written review between meetings is proposed. Its scope, channel and response times will be defined before enrollment opens; it does not include immediate responses or round-the-clock availability.

Future terms will explain the confidentiality agreement and its limits before participation. No agreement is currently in place and enrollment is not open.

A clear scope

It does not provide diagnosis, psychological treatment, couples therapy, or prescribed exercise or nutrition plans.

If a need falls outside the scope of this support, the appropriate step is to consult a qualified professional.
