    A respectful space

    • Voluntary participation and respect for different experiences and beliefs.
    • Do not diagnose others, give clinical advice or promise health outcomes. The community does not replace professional care.
    • Unsolicited promotion, sales and promotional direct messages are not allowed.
    • Do not share other people’s stories or private details, take screenshots or record conversations without their explicit consent.
    • We care about privacy, but cannot guarantee confidentiality in an online group. Share only what feels comfortable and avoid intimate or clinical information.
    • A weekly 45-minute live session is planned. The day, time, platform and link will be announced once confirmed.
