Terms for this stage.

This private version lets you review the project and use its free resources.

Resources and participation.

The seven-day workbook is free, with no obligation to subscribe. It offers reflection exercises for adults. You can adapt or skip the exercises and keep your responses private.

No personal, physical, financial or clinical outcomes are promised. The content does not replace professional health services.

Community in development.

Participation will be voluntary. The WhatsApp channel, dates and access arrangements are not yet confirmed. Before you join a group, you will be told what information, including your phone number, other participants may be able to see.

The planned format includes a weekly 45-minute session. The day, time and platform will be confirmed once people have expressed interest.

Mentoring and payments.

Individual and group mentoring are not yet available to purchase. This website does not take payments or reserve places. Before any future purchase, the service, price, duration, changes, cancellations and other applicable terms will be clearly presented for your review.

Conversation requests.

The prepared email and calendar file contain a pending proposal. The session is only agreed through a later confirmation. If you need to change your proposal, write through the same channel.
