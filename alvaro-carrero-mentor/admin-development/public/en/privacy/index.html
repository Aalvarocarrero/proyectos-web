Status of this private review version

Resource without signup

You can read or download the seven-day resource without providing your name, email or personal information. Your reflections are not sent to the website.

Signup with Kit

When you submit the form, you share your email address, confirmation that you are aged 18 or over, and selected options with Kit. Kit handles email confirmation, resource delivery and the updates you request. We do not ask for your date of birth or health information.

Session requests

The session form prepares an email in your app. It is only sent if you confirm it. The date and calendar file are a pending proposal, not a confirmed booking.

You choose your updates

General community, individual mentoring and group mentoring updates are selected separately, with no preselected options. The needs you indicate are interests, not permission to send other messages. Receiving the PDF does not require subscribing to updates: you can also download it directly without signing up.

Terms for this stage

Private feedback

The feedback area stores your answers in this website’s database so Álvaro can review them and improve the proposal. You can submit without a name or email; contact details are requested only if you would like a reply. Feedback does not subscribe you to updates and is not published as a testimonial.

Do not include medical, financial or other people’s private information. The hosting platform may retain access and security logs, so submitting without a name does not mean you cannot be identified. Original answers and contact details are deleted after 90 days by a daily cleanup job; physical deletion may occur at its next run. Only separate learning notes reviewed by Álvaro and stripped of identifiers are retained. Removing a name or email alone does not make free text anonymous. A temporary technical identifier derived from the IP address is used to limit abuse; the original IP is not stored in this database. Expired counters are removed on later requests or by the daily job. You can request deletion using the contact details provided.
